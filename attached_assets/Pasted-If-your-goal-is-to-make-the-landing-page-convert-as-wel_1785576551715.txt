* One-time payment

12. Security Logos

13. “Why Choose Map Lead Extractor?”

Instead of listing features, focus on benefits:

* Find prospects faster
* Save hours every week
* Organize your outreach
* Spend more time selling

⸻

14. Exit-Intent Popup

If someone is about to leave:

Wait!

Get $50 OFF today only.

Buy Now for $147

(Only use this if you’re genuinely willing to offer that discount.)

⸻

15. Add a “Results” Section

Show a realistic workflow:

* Search by industry
* Build a prospect list
* Export
* Contact businesses

This demonstrates how the software fits into a sales process without implying guaranteed business outcomes.

The three additions I’d prioritize first

1. A real dashboard demo video at the top.
2. A “Who It’s For” section with clear customer types.
3. A “What You Can Search” section listing dozens of industries.

Those additions make the product easier to understand and help visitors quickly decide whether it’s a fit for their business.
